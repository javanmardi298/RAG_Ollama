# Returns and the right of withdrawal

As a consumer in the EU you have a legal right of withdrawal: you can return an item within **14 days** of delivery without giving a reason.

We extend this voluntarily: you can return items within **30 days** of delivery. Return shipping is free; we send you a prepaid label.

Items that were **made to your specifications** (custom-made or personalised products) cannot be returned unless they are faulty.

A return can only be started for orders with the status **delivered**. Orders that are still on the way cannot be returned yet.
