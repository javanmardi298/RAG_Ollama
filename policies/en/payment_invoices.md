# Payment methods and invoices

We accept PayPal, credit card (Visa, Mastercard), SEPA direct debit and Klarna (pay later).

An invoice is sent by email when your order is shipped. You can request a copy of the invoice at any time with your order number.

Payment problems such as double charges or failed payments are handled by our team, not by the chatbot, because they need access to payment systems.
