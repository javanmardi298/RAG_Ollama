# Cancelling an order

You can cancel an order free of charge as long as its status is **processing**, which means it has not left our warehouse yet. There is no cancellation fee.

Once an order has been **shipped**, it can no longer be cancelled. Instead, wait for the delivery and return the item (see "Returns and the right of withdrawal").

To cancel, give us your order number and the email address you used for the order. After cancellation, the full amount is refunded to your original payment method.
