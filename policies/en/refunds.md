# Refunds

Refunds always go back to the **original payment method**. We issue the refund within **14 days** after the returned item arrives at our warehouse.

Refunds up to **150 EUR** are approved automatically. Larger refunds are checked by a member of our support team, which can take up to two working days longer.

You can ask us about the status of a refund at any time with your order number.
