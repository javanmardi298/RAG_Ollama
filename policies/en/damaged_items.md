# Damaged or faulty items

If an item arrives damaged, please tell us within **7 days** of delivery and send a photo of the damage.

For items worth up to **150 EUR** we send a replacement or refund right away. For more expensive items, our team reviews the case first.

Your statutory warranty rights for faulty goods are not affected by this policy.
