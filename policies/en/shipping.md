# Shipping, delivery and addresses

We ship within Germany with DHL or Hermes. Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 working days after the order leaves the warehouse; large furniture can take 7 to 10 working days.

You receive a tracking number as soon as your order is **shipped**.

The delivery address can be changed only while the order is **processing**. After it has been shipped, please contact the carrier directly with your tracking number.

If an order has been shipped for more than **10 days** and has not arrived, we treat it as delayed and investigate with the carrier.
