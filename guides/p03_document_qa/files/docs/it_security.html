IT Security Policy

Passwords

Passwords must have at least 12 characters. Never reuse a work password on another website. Use the company password manager.

Multi-factor authentication

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for email, VPN and the HR portal. Use the authenticator app; SMS codes are not allowed.

Phishing

Report suspicious emails with the "Report phishing" button. Do not forward them to colleagues. The security team answers within 4 working hours.

Laptops

Laptops are replaced every 3 years. A lost or stolen laptop must be reported to the IT service desk within 24 hours.
