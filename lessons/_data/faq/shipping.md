# Shipping

## Delivery times
Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 working days. Express delivery takes 1 to 2 working days and is available for small items.

## Costs
Shipping is free above 50 EUR. Below that, standard shipping costs 4.90 EUR and express costs 9.90 EUR.

## Tracking
You receive a tracking link by email when the parcel leaves our warehouse.
